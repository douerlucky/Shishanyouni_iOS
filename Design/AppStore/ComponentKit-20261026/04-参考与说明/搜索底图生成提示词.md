# 搜索底图生成提示词

使用内置 ImageGen；根据用户补充，改为低对比狮子山、南湖和校园入口的轮廓纹样，主体信息区保持留白。

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: one subtle low-contrast patterned BACKGROUND ONLY for an App Store search-results creative asset, used below separate title/UI/character layers in a Pixelmator PXD. Landscape 3:2 aspect ratio.
Input image 1: the user's campus entrance photograph is ONLY an architecture-shape reference for a tiny simplified gate motif. Do not reproduce the photograph, stone texture, inscription or lettering.
Primary request: a fresh pale lake-blue flat background with barely-there decorative LINE-ART CONTOURS that evoke Lion Mountain and South Lake at Huazhong Agricultural University. More character than a plain solid color, but quiet enough to read small dark text over it.
Base: uniform very pale lake blue, close to #EAF5FA, matte and flat, no gradient.
Decorative motifs, limited to the periphery:
- Lower left edge: two or three sparse gently rounded long hill-ridge outline curves suggesting Lion Mountain, using an extremely pale muted mint-blue ink.
- Along bottom 15 to 20 percent: three to five spacious smooth shoreline / lake-ripple contour lines evoking South Lake, in very pale muted blue. Soft rhythmic large-scale curves, not many repeated waves.
- Lower right corner / edge: a SMALL economical single-line outline of the campus entrance geometry from the reference, two square gate pillars and a long low blank central stone shape, in very pale desaturated blue-gray. Entire motif under 15 percent image width. NO writing on it.
- Optionally one tiny thin reed/leaf contour touching the far side edge, no flowers.
KEEP THE CENTRAL 65 PERCENT OF THE CANVAS CLEAR and nearly uniform, especially x=20%..80%, y=20%..75%, so this works as a base behind separately placed UI components and title text. Decoration must stay low contrast, at most about 8 percent darker than the pale base. No central object, no focal hero illustration, no foreground props.
Style: economical clean 2D Japanese everyday-life anime graphic-design motif; simple hand-inked contour lines and broad flat color fields; smooth lines with a little natural variation, wide spacing and fresh restrained palette. The 'texture' comes from campus line motifs, NOT from paper grain.
AVOID: photorealism, copies of the photo, painted sky scene, watercolor wash, brushed photo collage edges, paper fibers, canvas weave, noise, fabric texture, halftone, gradients, 3D, shadows, glossy effects, fancy ornament, particles or glitter. No logo, no words, no school names, no Chinese characters, no character name, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no mascot or people, no watermark, no phone frame, no border.
Output: one full-bleed opaque pale-background rectangle, landscape 3:2, high practical resolution. Decorative line art stays at the outside and the center is usable empty space.
```
